Executive summary

Generated 01 September 2026, 09:59
from 13 weeks of weekly operational data

Group service held broadly stable this quarter, but Technical Support has moved outside tolerance on 4 of its headline KPIs and is dragging the group Service Level to 77.8%. It needs a recovery plan before the next Board cycle.

Across 631,924 handled voice contacts, group Service Level weakened from 83.8% to 77.8% over the 13 weeks, with ASA at 36s and abandonment at 5.8%. The quarter-end position is outside the 80.0% access commitment.

Performance is uneven by unit. SME Desk is the strongest (93/100 on headline KPIs), while Technical Support is the weakest (36/100). The gap between them widened during the quarter and is now the single biggest driver of group-level variance.

On efficiency, AHT rose 9s and cost per contact is AED 20.01 (+0.13 AED vs quarter start). FCR at 78.8% means roughly 21% of contacts still generate a repeat — the largest single avoidable-volume opportunity.

Recommended actions

Owner and horizon assigned · tracked to next review

Stand up a Technical Support service-recovery cell

SL has fallen to 59.3% and abandonment to 10.9% in the last three weeks. Root-cause the split between attrition and the platform incident, add interim licensed overflow, and report daily until SL is back above target for five consecutive days.

Owner: Head of Technical Support / WFM Horizon: This week

Break the Collections AHT drift

AHT has risen 27s across the quarter (+3.1s/week) with no matching FCR gain — a coaching or process signal, not demand. Call-listen the longest 10% of contacts, refresh the disclosure script, and set a weekly AHT ceiling per team.

Owner: Collections Ops Manager / QA Horizon: 2–4 weeks

Move all units onto the standardised review template

This pack is generated from one KPI definition file and one layout. Retiring the five bespoke unit decks removes ~2 analyst-days per week and makes cross-unit comparison automatic.

Owner: Service Review & Insights Horizon: 30 days

Appendix — standardised KPI definitions

Single source of truth · applied identically to every unit
KPIDefinitionTargetRAG rule
Service Level Percentage of voice contacts answered within 20 seconds. Primary access commitment reported to the Board. 80.0% green ≥ 80.0% · red < 75.0%
Average Speed of Answer Mean time a caller waits in queue before connecting to an agent, including IVR-to-queue transfer time. 30s green ≤ 30s · red > 45s
Abandonment Rate Percentage of queued voice contacts that disconnect before reaching an agent. Excludes calls abandoned in under 5 seconds. 5.0% green ≤ 5.0% · red > 8.0%
Average Handle Time Talk time + hold time + after-call work per handled contact. Movement is read alongside FCR to avoid rewarding rushed calls. 360s green ≤ 360s · red > 400s
First Contact Resolution Percentage of contacts with no related repeat contact from the same customer within 7 calendar days. 78.0% green ≥ 78.0% · red < 72.0%
Customer Satisfaction Percentage of post-contact survey respondents scoring the interaction 4 or 5 out of 5. 85.0% green ≥ 85.0% · red < 80.0%
Repeat Contact Rate Percentage of contacts followed by a further contact from the same customer within 7 days. Inverse lens on FCR. 20.0% green ≤ 20.0% · red > 25.0%
Occupancy Share of logged-in time spent in contact-related work. Below 80% signals over-staffing; above 90% signals burnout / shrinkage risk. 80–90% green inside band
Schedule Adherence Percentage of scheduled on-phone time the agent was actually available, within a 5-minute tolerance. 90.0% green ≥ 90.0% · red < 85.0%
Forecast Accuracy 1 - MAPE of the weekly volume forecast against actual offered contacts. Drives roster reliability. 92.0% green ≥ 92.0% · red < 88.0%
Cost per Contact Fully-loaded contact centre operating cost divided by handled contacts for the period. AED 18.00 green ≤ AED 18.00 · red > AED 21.00
